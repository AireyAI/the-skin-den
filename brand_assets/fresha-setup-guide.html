Before you start

Why Fresha

It's free for you and free for your clients, it's built specifically for beauty and skincare businesses, and — most importantly — it takes a card-on-file deposit automatically at the point of booking. That means your existing 50% deposit and cancellation policy finally enforces itself instead of you having to chase it.

1

Create your account

Go to fresha.com → click "For business" → sign up with an email address. This can be a brand new one just for the business, or one you already use.

2

Set up your business profile

  • Business name — The Skin Den (by Rachel)
  • Category — Skin care / Facial studio / Acne treatment
  • Phone — 07568 602861
  • Address — 73 Greymoor Way, Carlisle, CA3 0FQ
Fresha has a privacy toggle so only the general area ("Carlisle, CA3") shows publicly, and your exact address stays hidden until someone actually books — matches what the website already says.

4

Turn on deposits

Go to Settings → No-show protection (sometimes called "Booking protection" or "Deposits") and turn on "Require card details / deposit for new clients", set to 50% of the service price.

48+ hrsFree reschedule
< 48 hrs50% charged
< 24 hrs / no-show100% charged
This is exactly the policy already on your website and Instagram — Fresha now enforces it automatically instead of you having to chase anyone.

5

Connect your payouts

Settings → Payments — connect your bank account so deposits land with you. This step is between you and Fresha directly; neither Kyle nor Claude ever sees your bank details. You may need to verify your ID — standard for any payment provider.

6

Set your working hours

Settings → Business hours — whatever days and times you actually take clients. This is what stops anyone booking a slot you're not available for.

7

Send back your booking link

Settings → Online booking (or Marketing → Sales channels) — copy your public booking link. It'll look something like fresha.com/book-now/the-skin-den-xxxxx.

That's the last piece

Send that link back to Kyle and your "Book a consultation" button goes live the same day — with real online booking and automatic deposits.
