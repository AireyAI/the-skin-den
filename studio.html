Asked & answered

Things worth knowing before you book.

I'm a new client — where do I start?
With the Skin Consultation & Advanced Facial (£50). It's a full consultation to understand your skin, your goals and your lifestyle — paired with a personalised advanced facial and a home-care plan you actually take home. Every plan after this is built on that conversation.
Will there be downtime?
Most facials and the Hydradermabrasion have no downtime. Stronger peels and microneedling typically come with 24–48 hours of redness and dryness. We'll talk you through what to expect — and what to avoid — at consultation, and you'll never be booked into something you're not prepared for.
How do you treat acne?
The Acne Consultation & Treatment (£65) starts with a proper assessment — triggers, lifestyle, contributing factors. From there we build a personalised plan. Often that includes a course of treatments stacked over time, not a one-and-done. We treat the cause, not just the spots.
Do I need to commit to a course?
No. Every treatment can be booked individually. Skin boosters are usually advised as a course of 3 for full effect, and acne plans often need stacking — but nothing is sold to you. You'll always know exactly what you're booking and why.
What's the deposit policy?
For first-time clients, a 50% deposit secures your booking. It's deducted from your treatment total on the day. Cancellations under 48 hours forfeit 50%; under 24 hours or no-show, 100%. See the full policy for detail.
Where are you based?
A private studio in Carlisle, Cumbria (CA3). The full address is shared on booking confirmation — appointments are one-to-one and unrushed.
I'm pregnant / have a skin condition / take certain meds — can I still book?
Please always disclose pregnancy, active skin conditions and medications when booking. Some treatments are contraindicated — but there's almost always a safe alternative. The consultation is exactly the place to figure that out.
