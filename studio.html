Meet Rachel

Meet Rachel.

I’m Rachel, a VTCT-qualified, fully insured skincare practitioner and acne specialist based in Carlisle.

I created The Skin Den for people who feel overwhelmed, confused or stuck with their skin and simply don’t know what to do next.

Skin can be complicated, and sometimes knowing where to start is the hardest part. My approach is to take the time to understand your skin, what it needs and what you want to achieve.

From there, we can create a clear, personalised approach focused on improving the health of your skin.

Whether you’re dealing with acne, congestion, dehydration, pigmentation, ageing or simply want to feel happier and healthier in your skin, you’re welcome at The Skin Den.

Every appointment is one-to-one and tailored to you, with treatments adapted to your skin and how it presents on the day.

My goal is simple: to help you understand your skin, feel confident in it and work towards healthier skin.
