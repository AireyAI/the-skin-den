# Public website visibility — Google and AI search

Checked 2 November 2026. Apply to new public sites, substantial redesigns, requested SEO work and relevant public-page releases. Small changes get affected-page checks. Private app work and daily content operations stay in their own lanes. Preserve existing approved stack, URLs, brand, providers and client facts.

## Establish intent and evidence
Identify the visitor, actual service/product, geography, primary action and public pages that deserve discovery. Reuse approved client notes and authorised search data. Label unmeasured demand assumptions. For meaningful design/research use the existing model routing and a bounded source-linked competitor brief. Explicit planning requests use opus-planner. Do not restart research for every edit.
Build original, useful content from actual experience and verified facts: service scope, process, suitable customers, constraints, real cost basis where known, original proof and a clear next action. Use readable sections, descriptive headings and relevant internal links. Clear answers, tables or FAQs are useful when the subject warrants them. Their shape or word count is not an established citation formula. Missing business facts stay unresolved. Bundle blocking questions; continue safe work with labelled draft content.
Keep business identity/contact, service areas, prices, availability, credentials and policies consistent across visible copy, structured data and authorised listings. Use genuine evidence. Avoid fabricated reviews, artificial mentions, bulk near-duplicate location pages, keyword stuffing, hidden bot instructions and bought-link schemes.

## Public-page delivery
Use stable, customer-meaningful URLs and real crawlable links. Check the actual built routes, response status and final destination. Primary information should be available without sign-in, a chatbot conversation, scrolling interaction or canvas-only rendering. Check initial HTML and browser-rendered content. Where the existing stack supports it, use static/server rendering for important public content when needed for crawler access; Google can render JavaScript, while other crawlers may not. Do not replace frameworks solely for an assumed ranking boost.
Verify titles/descriptions, canonical choices, redirects, meaningful 404 handling and internal-link/asset integrity. Align canonical public URLs with sitemap entries and truthful modification dates. Check robots.txt plus page/HTTP indexing and snippet controls on the actual deployment. Preserve valuable URLs or prepare suitable redirects for approved changes. Check generated build allowlists so new public routes/assets are included.
Keep private staff/member/customer data authenticated. Indexing directives do not provide access control. Protect previews and exclude them from indexing. A robots crawl block may prevent a crawler seeing noindex, so choose controls deliberately. Do not expose internal data to improve search visibility.
Use supported structured data only when it matches verified visible content. Select the current applicable feature guide, parse/validate markup and use existing feature-validation tools when available. Product/LocalBusiness/Organization/Breadcrumb markup depends on the actual business and page. No invented ratings, offers or local offices. Google does not require special AI schema or llms.txt. Do not add llms.txt or duplicate Markdown pages as a claimed Google ranking fix. An explicit non-Google consumer can justify them separately.

## AI-provider access policy
Record the owner's intended public search access and separate training choice. Retain documented preferences. Prepare proposed changes when policy is unknown; continue local building and flag the decision. Check robots rules, snippet/header directives, provider account controls and CDN/WAF challenges. Use provider-current verification methods and published ranges if a scoped exception is necessary. A spoofed User-Agent request alone does not verify real crawler access. Preserve authentication, private-route exclusions and existing security protections; account/WAF changes require concrete approval.
Google uses Googlebot and normal search eligibility for Search AI features. Newer Google guidance also identifies a Search Console generative-AI inclusion control. Review the current setting where authorised. Google-Extended governs other documented Google AI uses; inspect its current scope before changing it.
OpenAI: OAI-SearchBot supports search discovery; GPTBot concerns training; ChatGPT-User performs requested retrieval. Anthropic: Claude-SearchBot supports search, Claude-User requested retrieval, ClaudeBot potential training collection. PerplexityBot supports search, Perplexity-User requested retrieval. These purposes differ; do not blanket-enable every AI bot. For Bing/Copilot inspect current independent training/grounding preferences and the October 2026 transition guidance before using older NoArchive/NoCache recipes.

## Local, commerce and user experience
For local businesses check verified category, address/service area, hours, contacts, original photos and genuine review processes. Propose Business Profile corrections through authorised account work. Only build location pages with distinct value and actual service coverage. For shops verify product/offer facts, shipping/returns and relevant Merchant Center consistency. Marketplace identity must reflect the real platform and partners.
Carry the existing accessibility, mobile and performance checks into the build. Optimise images/dimensions, fonts, loading priority and unnecessary JavaScript. Respect reduced motion and keep primary content accessible around overlays. Check the enquiry/booking journey through its intended destination in a safe test context. Report lab measurements separately from field data. Core Web Vitals goals remain p75 LCP <=2.5s, INP <=200ms, CLS <=0.1. Good scores support experience; they do not guarantee a ranking.

## Evidence and measurement
For the applicable scope report pass/fail/inapplicable/unresolved with page/tool evidence for content facts, public/private separation, status/rendering, links, canonicals/sitemap, indexing/snippet controls, AI access policy, structured data, mobile/performance and conversion path. Missing account tools are unresolved checks, not permission to claim search-ready. Local work can proceed with these gaps recorded.
After approved publication verify hosted artifacts separately. Use authorised Google Search Console indexing/performance and generative-AI reports, plus Bing Webmaster AI Performance where available. Newer 2026 Google reporting guidance supersedes older Web-only descriptions; check the property's actual views. Track impressions, citations, visits and qualified conversions as different outcomes. A citation does not prove a visit or enquiry. Record date/query/location conditions for manual AI observations and avoid attributing every change to a single edit.
Consider existing IndexNow support for meaningful URL additions/updates/removals on participating engines. It complements sitemap discovery and does not guarantee indexing. Do not introduce automatic model monitors, paid SEO tools or repetitive unchanged submissions. Never guarantee first position, a citation or first-pass ranking success.
