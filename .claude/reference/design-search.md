# Design research and search visibility

Use for new public websites, substantial redesigns or requested SEO work. Small fixes skip fresh research. Preserve useful existing URLs, content and brand equity.

## Research a direction
Use the existing browser tooling to inspect a bounded selection of real sites. Start with supplied references, then typically two relevant competitors and one exceptional design reference. Search results and award galleries help discover candidates. Inspect the actual website on desktop and mobile before describing its visual execution. A screenshot or search snippet alone does not establish the booking flow or accessibility.
Record URLs, date, visitor goal, useful observations and proposed adaptations in a compact project note. Look at hierarchy, typography, spacing, image direction, navigation, proof, pricing clarity, calls to action and interaction states. Separate direct observation from inference. Extract principles and build an original client-specific design using approved/licensed assets. Avoid copying competitors' text, branding or images. Respect site access restrictions.
Translate Kyle's preference for Astra-quality design into observable choices: coherent art direction, strong composition, disciplined type and spacing, purposeful imagery, responsive behaviour and complete states. Use supplied examples when available. No specific look can be inferred solely from a model name.
Give designer a short synthesis, actual client assets and one direction. Research again only when evidence is missing or the brief changes. Keep useful source-linked references in the project for later work.

## Search visibility
For new public builds, major public redesigns, SEO/AI-search requests and relevant launches, read `.claude/reference/search-visibility.md`. Apply its content, crawler-policy, indexing and evidence checks proportionally. Reuse existing research for small edits.

## Research handoff
For substantial new website/dashboard design, the parent reuses existing source-linked research or commissions design-researcher (Sonnet/medium) before briefing designer (Opus/xhigh). Haiku docs-researcher handles bounded factual extraction from supplied official URLs. Do not spawn Haiku for trivial lookups the parent can do directly. The parent passes a compact research brief to designer, owns saved project notes and prevents repeated browsing. Agents never spawn one another. Small edits skip fresh competitor research. No idle research or scheduled inference. Escalate a missing browser capability explicitly.
