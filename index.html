Meet Rachel.

I’m Rachel, a VTCT-qualified, fully insured skincare practitioner and acne specialist based in Carlisle.

I created The Skin Den for people who feel overwhelmed, confused or stuck with their skin. Whether it’s acne, congestion, dehydration, pigmentation or ageing, I take the time to understand your skin and what you want to achieve.

My goal is simple: to help you understand your skin, feel confident in it and work towards healthier skin.
